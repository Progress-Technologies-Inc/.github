### Nestides Tide Chart for WordPress

[Nestides Tide Chart](https://wordpress.org/plugins/nestides-tide-chart/) adds NOAA tide times and a tide chart to a WordPress site with one shortcode.

- High and low tide times and heights for U.S. NOAA stations, shown as a table and a chart
- 1 to 30 days per display
- A station-picker map in the admin that writes the shortcode for you
- Several stations on one page, in pages, posts and widgets
- Free plan with a monthly limit; paid plans raise the limit

Get it on [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/nestides-tide-chart/) · Learn more at [nestides.com](https://nestides.com/)

Tide data is NOAA-derived and delivered through the Nestides hosted service. NOAA is not affiliated with, and does not endorse, Nestides.
